Respect the child.

Petite Fleur is built around a simple belief: children are not empty vessels waiting to be filled. They arrive full of questions, ideas, and a desire to do things for themselves.

Our Montessori approach honours that drive. Adults prepare a calm, inviting environment, introduce meaningful work, and give children the space and support to explore it at their own pace.
